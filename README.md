# AfyaSalama

**One price for everyone: 500 TZS a week keeps the whole community safe from high blood pressure and diabetes.**

AfyaSalama turns trusted VICOBA savings groups in Tanzania into health groups. Every member pays the same 500 TZS a week by mobile money and receives the same care.

## How it works
1. **Member:** pays 500 TZS a week from a basic phone (USSD/SMS, Swahili and English) and checks the group health pot. Any member can add an optional solidarity top-up.
2. **Community health worker:** runs weekly blood pressure and sugar checks and records the readings. Members who keep their readings controlled pay less.
3. **Partners:** employers, insurers and local government pay only for verified health results, shown on an anonymous dashboard.

The shared health pot pays for health insurance enrollment, medicines and clinic transport.

## About this prototype
`index.html` is a clickable demo with **sample data**. It shows the member phone menu, the health worker check, and the partner dashboard. Open it in any browser; no install needed.

**Status:** interactive prototype. It has not yet been piloted with real users.

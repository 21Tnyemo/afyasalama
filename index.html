One price for everyone: 500 TZS a week keeps the whole community safe from high blood pressure and diabetes. This is a clickable demo with sample data. Follow the 3 steps below.

  1. Step 1: MemberPays 500 TZS a week from a basic phone and checks the health pot.
  2. Step 2: Health workerChecks blood pressure every week and records it.
  3. Step 3: PartnerEmployers, insurers and local government pay only when more people are healthy.

Neema's phone

Payment is simulated. In real use it goes by M-Pesa, Mixx by Yas or Airtel Money. The health pot pays for insurance enrollment, medicines and clinic transport.

Weekly health check

A trained community health worker meets the group each week. Everyone gets the same free check.
